常見問題

按播放沒有聲音?

先點一下畫面(iPhone 要在點擊之後才會開始出聲),再確認音量。網頁版第一次播放要先從網路下載鼓聲,下載完才會有聲音;App 版的鼓聲內建在 App 裡。iPhone 側邊的靜音開關打開時 App 照樣有聲音;如果還是沒聲音,請回報並附上裝置型號。

不連網可以用嗎?

App 版:可以,第一次打開就不需要網路。網頁版:連網打開過一次、各分頁的鼓聲下載過之後,就能離線使用。

檢定的要求跟官方一樣嗎?

技巧、視奏、聽力的要求照 Trinity Rock & Pop 與 RSL Rockschool 爵士鼓的官方大綱整理,每一項標出頁碼;大綱沒寫的地方標「推估」或「練習設定」。視奏、聽力的題目是 App 依大綱參數產生的練習題,不是官方題目,練習回饋也不換算成官方分數。僅供練習,實際要求以官方大綱為準。本 App 與 Trinity、RSL 沒有任何關係。

基本打法的出處?

照 Percussive Arts Society《International Drum Rudiments》官方表(40 個),左右手、重音、裝飾音都照官方寫法,鼓譜由 App 重新繪製。

練習紀錄存在哪裡?會上傳嗎?

只存在這台裝置上,不會上傳;刪除 App 或清除網站資料就會一起刪除。詳見隱私權政策。

換手機怎麼帶走練習紀錄?

在舊手機打開 App,捲到畫面最下面按「備份紀錄」→「存成檔案」,把檔案存到「檔案」或傳給自己。新手機打開 App、按「開始練習」,一樣捲到最下面按「備份紀錄」→「從檔案還原」,選那個檔案就好。

怎麼換考試、級數,或不考了?

打開「檢定」分頁,在上面的考試設定選考試系統與級數,或選「不考試」。

怎麼切換中英文、淺色/深色?

FAQ

No sound when I press play?

Tap the screen once (iPhone only starts audio after a tap) and check the volume. The web version downloads the drum sounds the first time you play; the app has them built in. The app still plays with the iPhone's silent switch on; if you still hear nothing, please report it with your device model.

Does it work offline?

App: yes, from the very first launch. Web: after you have opened it online once and each tab's drum sounds have downloaded.

Are the exam requirements official?

Technique, sight-reading and aural requirements follow the official Trinity Rock & Pop and RSL Rockschool drums syllabuses, with a page reference for each item; anything the syllabus leaves open is marked "Assumed" or "Practice setting". Sight-reading and aural tests are generated by the app from the syllabus parameters — they are not official tests, and practice feedback is never converted into exam marks. Practice only — the official syllabus is final. This app is not affiliated with Trinity or RSL.

Where do the rudiments come from?

The Percussive Arts Society International Drum Rudiments (all 40), with sticking, accents and grace notes as in the official sheet, re-engraved by the app.

Where is my practice history kept? Is it uploaded?

Only on this device, never uploaded; it is removed when you delete the app or clear site data. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my practice history to a new phone?

On the old phone, open the app, scroll to the very bottom and tap “Backup” → “Save to file”, then save the file to Files or send it to yourself. On the new phone, open the app, tap “Start practicing”, scroll to the bottom and tap “Backup” → “Restore from file”, and pick that file.

How do I change exam or grade, or stop exam prep?

Open the Exams tab and pick a board and grade at the top, or choose "No exam".

How do I switch language or light/dark theme?

The two buttons at the right of the top bar.
