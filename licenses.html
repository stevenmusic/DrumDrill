Drum sounds

Naked Drums by Wilkinson Audio, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Changes: the samples were organised and renamed by ScrollScore; during playback this app adds room reverb, compression and limiting, sets a volume for each kit piece, lets the hi-hat cut itself off (a closed or foot hi-hat stops an open one), limits how many hits of one cymbal ring at once, and mixes the two adjacent layers of a drum at velocity boundaries; the metronome plays only the first 0.4 seconds of each stick click; the app version includes only part of the recordings (the first few round-robins of each velocity layer).
