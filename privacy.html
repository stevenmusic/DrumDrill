隱私權政策

DrumDrill 爵士鼓檢定特訓 · 最後更新 2026-10-18

我們不收集任何個人資料

DrumDrill 不需要註冊、不需要登入,沒有廣告、沒有分析追蹤,也不會把任何資料傳到我們的伺服器(我們沒有伺服器)。

存在你裝置上的資料

  • 練習設定、考試級數、通過標記、各打法示範播完的最快速度與練習紀錄:只存在這台裝置的本機儲存空間,刪除 App 或清除網站資料就會一起刪除。
  • 頁尾的「備份紀錄」存下來的檔案、「分享本週摘要」的文字:只在你的裝置上產生,由你自己決定存在哪裡、傳給誰;App 不會上傳。
  • 鼓的取樣音檔:App 版內建在 App 裡,不需要連網。網頁版從 GitHub(raw.githubusercontent.com)下載:技巧的小鼓在第一次播放時下載,視奏、聽力用的整組鼓(大鼓、小鼓、筒鼓、hi-hat、鈸等 12 個鼓件)在第一次打開這兩個分頁時下載;下載過的檔案存在裝置上供離線使用(離開視奏、聽力一段時間後會從記憶體釋放,但裝置上的檔案還在,下次不用再下載)。下載時 GitHub 會看到一般的網路連線資訊(IP 位址等),適用 GitHub 的隱私權政策。

網頁版的字型

網頁版從 Google Fonts(fonts.googleapis.com、fonts.gstatic.com)載入字型(Noto Sans TC、Noto Serif TC),載入時 Google 會看到一般的網路連線資訊(IP 位址、瀏覽器等),適用 Google 的隱私權政策。App 版不載入 Google Fonts,改用裝置內建的字型,不會連到 Google。

麥克風(選用;目前版本未開放)

目前版本不會使用麥克風(這個功能暫時關閉,之後的版本才會開放)。開放以後的做法如下:

視奏與聽力的「打一遍」可以選擇用麥克風評分節奏。只有你勾選並允許之後才會使用;聲音只在這台裝置上分析每一下敲擊的時間,不錄音存檔、不上傳,打完就丟掉。不勾選就完全不會用到麥克風。

兒童

本 App 適合所有年齡,不收集兒童的任何資料。

回報問題

App 不會自動傳送任何資料(沒有當機回報、沒有分析)。在支援頁按「回報」時,才會打開預先填好 App 版本、裝置、語言的 GitHub 表單或郵件,你看過、自己按送出才會送出;不包含練習紀錄。GitHub 上的 issue 是公開的。

聯絡

問題或建議請看支援頁,或到 GitHub:github.com/stevenmusic/DrumDrill

Privacy Policy

DrumDrill · Last updated 2026-10-18

We collect no personal data

DrumDrill needs no account or sign-in, shows no ads, uses no analytics, and sends nothing to any server of ours (there is none).

Data stored on your device

  • Settings, your exam and grade, pass marks, best tempos and the practice log are kept only in this device's local storage and are removed when you delete the app or clear site data.
  • The backup file from “Backup” and the text from “Share this week” (at the bottom of the page) are made on your device; you choose where to save them and whom to send them to. The app uploads nothing.
  • Drum samples are built into the app and need no connection. The web version downloads them from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): the snare for Technique the first time you play it, and the full kit for Sight-reading and Aural (12 pieces: kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, cymbals and more) the first time you open those tabs. Downloaded files are kept on the device for offline use (they are released from memory a while after you leave those tabs, but stay on the device so they are not downloaded again). GitHub sees ordinary connection information (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Fonts on the web version

The web version loads its fonts (Noto Sans TC, Noto Serif TC) from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), so Google sees ordinary connection information (such as your IP address and browser) under its own privacy policy. The app does not load Google Fonts; it uses the fonts built into your device and never connects to Google.

Microphone (optional; not in the current version)

The current version never uses the microphone (the feature is switched off for now). When it returns, it works as follows:

“Play it” in Sight-reading and Aural can optionally score your rhythm with the microphone. It is used only after you tick that option and allow access; the sound is analysed on this device for stroke times only, never recorded to a file or uploaded, and discarded when you finish. If you don't tick it, the microphone is never used.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children.

Reporting problems

The app never sends anything by itself (no crash reporting, no analytics). Only when you press Report on the support page does it open a GitHub form or email draft pre-filled with the app version, device and language; nothing is sent until you review it and send it yourself, and no practice history is included. GitHub issues are public.

Contact

Questions or suggestions: see the support page or github.com/stevenmusic/DrumDrill
