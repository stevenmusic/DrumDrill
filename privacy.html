隱私權政策

DrumDrill 爵士鼓檢定特訓 · 最後更新 2026-10-17

我們不收集任何個人資料

DrumDrill 不需要註冊、不需要登入,沒有廣告、沒有分析追蹤,也不會把任何資料傳到我們的伺服器(我們沒有伺服器)。

存在你裝置上的資料

  • 練習設定、考試級數、通過標記、各打法練過的最快速度與練習紀錄:只存在這台裝置的本機儲存空間,刪除 App 或清除網站資料就會一起刪除。
  • 鼓的取樣音檔:App 版內建在 App 裡,不需要連網。網頁版第一次播放時從 GitHub(raw.githubusercontent.com)下載,之後存在裝置上供離線使用;下載時 GitHub 會看到一般的網路連線資訊(IP 位址等),適用 GitHub 的隱私權政策。

兒童

本 App 適合所有年齡,不收集兒童的任何資料。

聯絡

Privacy Policy

DrumDrill · Last updated 2026-10-17

We collect no personal data

DrumDrill needs no account or sign-in, shows no ads, uses no analytics, and sends nothing to any server of ours (there is none).

Data stored on your device

  • Settings, your exam and grade, pass marks, best tempos and the practice log are kept only in this device's local storage and are removed when you delete the app or clear site data.
  • Drum samples are built into the app and need no connection. The web version downloads them from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) the first time they are played and caches them on the device for offline use; GitHub sees ordinary connection information (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children.
